Common questions

Will I be charged during the free trial?

No. You add a payment method, and we email you before the first payment. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

Can I book hotels or tickets here?

Not yet. We help you decide where to go and when, and link to the place's own website to book. We don't take commission, so our advice stays neutral.

Can I plan a trip with friends?

Yes, on both plans. Invite friends to a trip, plan the days together, and split the costs. Free trips can have up to 4 people.

How do you choose which places to recommend?

We look at crowd patterns, travel time, places our team has checked, and local businesses nearby. We never rank places by how viral they are, and businesses can't pay to appear.

Do businesses see my questions?

No. They only see combined trends from many travelers, never your name or your chats. You can switch this off in settings.
